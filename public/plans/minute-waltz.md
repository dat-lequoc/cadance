# Minute Waltz practice route

This self-directed route has ten merged checkpoints. Every checkpoint covers a complete musical section with both hands; there are no separate right- or left-hand quests. Choose any section in any order, or use the list as a progressive path. Ten completed runs are required per checkpoint. Start at half source tempo (quarter = 140) and raise it when the section is secure. The held trill bridge and final flourish stay inside their surrounding sections. Source MIDI does not realize every printed trill/prall; rehearse ornament technique separately.
