# Mozart K. 545 I preparation

Source: Mutopia Project 998, *Sonata Facile* first movement, sourced from IMSLP and typeset by Alejandro Sierra; the source is licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. The preserved score is `public/pieces/mozart-piano-sonata-k545-i.pdf`; the matching MIDI is `public/pieces/mozart-piano-sonata-k545-i.mid`.

The normalized piece has 1,288 notes, separate upper/lower source-staff targets, 73 playable MIDI measures, and 131 unlocked quests. The PDF engraving prints one additional final barline segment while the supplied MIDI ends at the preceding tick boundary; the final printed system is therefore kept intact and mapped to the 73 playable measure intervals rather than inventing silent timing.

Preparation can be reproduced with `scripts/mozart-k545-i-prepare.ts` and `scripts/mozart-k545-i-plan.ts`. Score crops are in `public/scores/mozart-piano-sonata-k545-i/` and reviewed cuts in `scripts/scores/mozart-piano-sonata-k545-i.cuts.json`. Following is bar-level for this edition; note anchors were not generated because one later system lacks the extractor's two-clef signature.
